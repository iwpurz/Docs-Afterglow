Page

Heads up: Dark-ify recolors the whole page, so photos, GIFs and emoji in your document can look inverted or tinted. Keep photos natural (below) softens this, and Crisp mode leaves pictures exactly as they are.

Recolors page text as Docs paints it instead of filtering the whole page, so photos, GIFs and emoji keep their real colors and text is not blurred. Reload your open documents after changing it. If it can't work on your Docs version it switches itself off.

If the page turns into a solid black or white block when you lower its opacity, try Screen (dark themes) or Multiply (light themes).

Accents

Typing effects

Themes can bring their own effect, sound and music. They only pick which one plays: your on/off switches below never change by themselves.

Drawn on a click-through layer at the cursor. Particle effects turn off automatically if your system asks for reduced motion; the simple fades stay on. Fade in works best on a plain (solid) page color.

Typing sounds

Every sound is generated live by your browser: no audio files, no downloads. The extension only sorts each key press into letter, space, Enter or Backspace to choose the sound. It never records, stores or sends what you type. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+S.

Music

    YouTube, Spotify and SoundCloud links show a small player in the corner of your document (drag it by its top bar); that site loads it, not this extension, and Spotify plays full songs only if you are signed in to Spotify in Firefox. Direct audio links and your own files play from the extension itself. Music starts on your first key press or click in the document. Files you add stay on this device. Shortcut: Alt+Shift+M.
